2. **Starting point of the flash check.** The recorder sees the root at `readystatechange: interactive` and later. It does not observe the document before that, so this shows no wrong appearance at first paint, not at every earlier instant.

4. **Sign-off.** Needs the owner to accept the exact-version peer decision (below) and to tick the task.

## Decision to confirm

Pinning `react-aria-components` and `react-aria` exactly means a consumer who already uses a different `react-aria` version gets a peer-dependency conflict instead of silent duplication. The alternative is ranges plus a documented override. I chose exact pins because duplication fails quietly (a nested-theme dialog renders in the wrong theme). Say if you want ranges instead.
